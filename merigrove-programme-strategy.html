Product overview
Strategic context

A third product tier. Non-linear revenue. A qualification engine.

The current DFY and DWY model requires senior consultant hours per client. Revenue grows linearly with headcount. This programme breaks that constraint, delivers the Merigrove Methodology to a cohort simultaneously, and creates a pipeline of pre-educated consulting candidates.

Lead-to-client workflow
Acquisition and conversion

From cold lead to enrolled client — with a consulting fork in the road.

Every inbound lead enters the same top of funnel. After the audit, the path splits: clients who meet the consulting criteria are pitched DFY or DWY first. Those who do not, or who choose the self-directed route, are offered the programme. No lead leaves without a clear, relevant next step.

Top of funnel — shared pathway
1
Organic content via Kacper's audience
Short-form content demonstrating the methodology in action. Case studies, teardowns, tactical posts. LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram. Each piece drives to the audit or the lead magnet.
2
Lead magnet capture
Free audit, unit economics calculator, sales management OS template, or 90-day revenue plan framework. Tactical enough to be valuable — vague enough to create a genuine need for the full methodology. Captures contact details into Merigrove CRM.
Lead magnet acts as a soft qualifier. High-engagement leads (those who use the tools) are more likely to book the audit call.
3
Automated business audit (Claude)
Lead completes the automated Merigrove audit. Scored diagnostic across all five methodology stages: Revenue Growth, Revenue Scalability, Revenue Predictability, Operational Efficiency, Financial Efficiency. Produces a personalised report with prioritised findings.
The audit does the qualification. Clients who score well overall but have one or two areas of acute weakness are ideal consulting prospects. Those with broader gaps across multiple stages are ideal programme clients.
4
Audit review call with Caleb
30-minute call to walk through audit findings. Radical candour: here is exactly what your scores mean, here is the priority order. This is where the path forks.
The fork: Caleb qualifies for consulting or programme based on the audit output and the conversation. Consulting criteria: revenue above $1m, clear operational capacity for DWY, or urgent need for DFY execution. Below threshold or self-directed preference: programme.
Post-fork · Two distinct paths
A
Path A: Consulting pitch (DFY or DWY)
Caleb flags the client as a consulting candidate. Merigrove leadership presents the appropriate consulting engagement — DFY for clients needing execution, DWY for those with internal capacity. Audit findings form the basis of the proposal.
If the consulting pitch does not convert (price, timing, preference for self-direction), the programme is immediately presented as the alternative. No lead should fall off entirely after an audit call.
B
Path B: Programme enrolment
Caleb presents the programme as the structured path to implement what the audit identified. "We've shown you where the gaps are. Here is the system that closes them — and you build it with our guidance." Stripe payment link sent. Contact data captured to Merigrove CRM.
5
5-month programme delivery
Cohort progresses through the curriculum. Dual-slot group coaching calls. Community, workbooks, and portal content. Wins captured in community channel for ongoing social proof. Client results documented as case studies.
6
Programme close and ascension offer
Closing audit run. Progress reviewed against opening baseline. Results shared with each client. Remaining gap identified — and the DWY or DFY consulting offer made to qualifying business owners as the natural next step. High-performing sales managers offered associate or ambassador placement.
Clients arrive at this moment already fluent in the methodology and aligned to the Merigrove framework. Consulting conversion is materially faster and easier than a cold pitch.

Risks and watch points
Operational risks

What to watch for before and during launch.

Delivery risk

Single-facilitator dependency on Caleb

Programme delivery is concentrated in Caleb. If he is unavailable, sick, or exits the arrangement, delivery fails. Mitigate by ensuring all call recordings, slide decks, and workbooks are owned by Merigrove. A substitute facilitator should be briefed and capable of covering at two weeks notice. Include a minimum notice clause in any agreement.

Distribution risk

Kacper's audience composition

If Kacper's following skews toward earlier-stage or lower-revenue audiences than the primary ICP, conversion on a $12,000 programme will be lower than expected. Validate with 2–3 posts and measure click-through and audit completions before committing to paid acquisition. The automated audit acts as a natural filter — unqualified leads self-select out before booking a call with Caleb.

Content risk

July launch is tight on production

10 weeks to launch with a live cohort model is achievable only if the team accepts the portal will not be finished on day one. Be transparent at enrolment: clients receive live delivery first, with portal recordings following each chapter within two weeks. Frame this as a founding member advantage — direct facilitator access during the formative run — not a limitation.

Conversion risk

Ascension requires a defined handoff process

Caleb identifies ascension candidates through delivery, but should not make the consulting pitch himself. The transition should come from Merigrove leadership, with Caleb facilitating the warm introduction. Without a defined handoff at a specific moment (recommended: end of month 4 group call), ascension will be ad hoc and lower-converting. Define the process before cohort 1 begins.

Commercial risk

Minimum viable cohort threshold

Below 8 enrolled clients the group dynamic suffers materially. If July arrives with fewer than 8 confirmed enrolments, delay by four weeks rather than run a thin cohort. A poor first cohort is more damaging than a delayed launch. The founding member discount and Kacper's reach should comfortably exceed this threshold — but set the internal go/no-go number clearly before launch.

Platform risk

Data and payment ownership

All payment data must route through Stripe directly to Merigrove. All client contact data must be captured in Merigrove's own CRM before or at the point of enrolment. Never allow the community platform (Skool or otherwise) to own the primary payment or contact relationship. This protects the business from platform dependency and enables direct re-marketing for future cohorts and consulting ascension.
